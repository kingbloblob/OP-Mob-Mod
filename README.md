# One Piece Bosses

A companion mod for the One Piece Mod that adds original rogue boss fights, plus the villains of the One Piece films (who aren't in the main mod's story), so there's always someone new to fight. Each boss brings their own crew, their own lines, a phase 3 signature move and a lair out in the world.

Requires the One Piece Mod (Fabric, Minecraft 26.3, Java 25).

## Their looks

Each rogue has their own body, with wild One Piece proportions instead of the player shape: Rourke is all barrel chest and ground-scraping forearms on tiny legs with his anchor on his back, Corvina is a stilt-legged shadow under a table-wide hat trailing a fog-grey train, Graves is a V-shaped slab with a ship's-prow chin and a torn Justice coat, Tsurugi is floating samurai armour with nothing inside, Gantz is a riveted boiler with a firebox belly, smokestacks and piston arms, and Nereus is a long-armed fishman with a dorsal fin, fin ears and a shark's jaw. Khamsin is a dune of a torso with a little turbaned head sunk between gorilla arms wrapped in chain and broken shackles; Gale is a skeleton under a tricorne twice his width, coins stuck in his ribs, a gold hook and a peg leg; Vasco rides a young Sea King (its neck and fanged head rear in front of him, its flippers walk it, its tail trails behind); and Kreuz is a shrivelled old man with a cranium three sizes too big, his brain in a glass dome on top, a stolen Pacifista arm, walker stilts for legs and two mechanical arms reaching over his head. They still walk, swing and look around like any mob, and coats, trains and skirts swing as they move. Each one's hitbox is sized to their body (`hitbox` in `bosses.json`).

The models and their skins come from `tools/gen_models.py` (needs Pillow): change a box or colour there and run `python3 tools/gen_models.py .`. It writes `client/RogueModels.java` and the 128x128 skins together, so they always match.

## How the fights work

- **Underneath they're the One Piece Mod's own bosses**, so everything its story bosses do, rogues do too: the health bar, phases at 66% and 33%, warning circles before attacks, Expert rage, healing when you run, Berries and XP, and Hard and Expert rematches with better prizes. Their special attacks are the main mod's boss powers.
- **Their crew** arrives when the fight starts, again at phase 2 and one last time at phase 3 (more on Hard and Expert, and the boss shouts its last call), and scatters when the boss falls. Crews are the main mod's pirate NPCs, so they use its fighting styles and skins. `/npc spawn scrapdeck_raider` and friends work too.
- **Their lines**: each rogue has something to say when the fight starts, at each phase and when beaten.
- **Bounty**: a first win raises your bounty by the rogue's own amount (12M for Rourke up to 600M for Nereus Vane). Rematches add 10% of the tier value, like any boss. The paper reports first wins, and Arsenal's meito count rogues as named bosses.
- **Trophy**: each rogue drops their trophy the first time someone nearby beats them at that difficulty.
- **Lair key**: each rogue drops the key to their lair's chest every time they're beaten ("Key to The Gran Tesoro" and so on). The chest is locked: hold the key in your main hand to open it. Without it the chest won't open or break, except in creative.
- **Lair Restoration Charter**: craft one (gold ingots around a diamond, paper and a compass: `GDG / PCP / GPG`) and use it inside a lair once its boss is beaten. The whole lair is rebuilt where it stands: every block, the boss and crew, and a fresh locked chest with new loot. It's used up. It also works on the One Piece Mod's boss structures whose boss lives in the structure (Buggy's Big Top, Loguetown, Rain Dinners, Shandora, Dressrosa Palace, Onigashima, Whole Cake Chateau and the rest listed in `opbosses/rebuildable.json`, from `tools/gen_rebuildable.py`).

## Their arenas

Every lair fights back. What each one does is in `src/main/resources/opbosses/arenas.json`.

- **Battle themes**: each lair has its own, written for this mod (Rourke's accordion stomp over clanking chains, Corvina's music box in the fog, Tsurugi's flute and taiko, Gantz's anvil beat, Tesoro's casino big band, Uta's concert, Ratchet's ticking clockwork...). It plays through the One Piece Mod's music player whenever you're near the boss, fading in and looping like its own boss themes, and the victory fanfare still plays when the boss falls. They follow the Music volume slider. The themes come from `tools/gen_music.py`, which uses the main mod's own synthesizer: `ONEPIECE_SOUNDS=<one-piece-mod>/tools/sounds python3 tools/gen_music.py [lair ...]`.
- **Boss bar**: the bar takes the lair's colour (green for the Scrapheap Reef, gold for the Gran Tesoro, pink for Uta's stage...). Corvina's and Gale's fights bring the bar's thick fog down on you, and Tsurugi, Nereus and Byrnndi World darken the sky in phase 3. Hard and Expert keep the main mod's notched bar.
- **Hazards**: from phase 2, and a second one from phase 3, the lair throws something at the players in the fight every few seconds (faster in phase 3 and on Hard and Expert). Everything is marked first, with a red ring, crumbling blocks or a bubbling vent about 1.5 seconds before it lands:
  - **collapse**: the floor caves in under you. Only ever a shallow pit (never over caves, water or lava, never chests or unbreakable blocks), and it closes again 10 seconds later or the moment the boss falls, lifting anyone still in it.
  - **debris**: rubble, cannon shells, gold or candy falls on the marked spots.
  - **geyser**: a vent erupts under you and throws you up (steam, stage pyrotechnics, lava, molten gold).
  - **cloud**: a lingering cloud (Corvina's lantern smoke blinds, Tsurugi's cursed mist withers, Kreuz's gas poisons, Uta's song spins the stage).
  - **lightning**: marked bolts (they never set the lair on fire).
  - **tide**: a shockwave from the boss throws everyone back (Nereus's waves, El Drago's roar, Byrnndi World's fist).
  - **fog**: darkness washes over the arena now and then.

  Each hazard announces itself once in chat when it starts ("The rotten scrap deck starts to give way!").

## Finding them

- **Rumor Posters** are the rogues' Wanted Posters. Use one on a block to call the rogue out. Beat them and the poster brings them back on Hard, then Expert; sneak while using it for a Normal fight.
- The main mod's pirates and Marines sometimes carry a Rumor Poster when beaten (4% for a tier 1 rogue down to 0.6% for tier 5). Which NPCs know about which rogue is `rumorFrom` in `bosses.json`.
- Creative: the **One Piece: Rogue Bosses** tab has every poster, trophy, key and lair chest (a chest you place yourself is plain storage, with no lock).

## Out in the world

Two kinds of mobs that fill gaps the One Piece Mod leaves open. Its own Marine squads, Devil Fruit bounty hunters (from 100M), Sea Kings, Pacifista hunts and pirate ships and patrols are untouched, so nothing here doubles them.

- **Wandering bounty hunters** live off small bounties. If yours is under 100M, every so often one to three of them pick up your trail (a veteran leads them from 50M), call you out and come for you. Over 100M you're out of their league and they leave you alone. Without a bounty you'll still meet pairs walking the land; they don't bother you, but they pick fights with pirate NPCs and raiders. Beating one raises your bounty by 2M to 4M (5M for a veteran). They're on the law's side, so they never fight Marines.
- **Rookie pirate raids.** While you're in a village (three or more villagers nearby) or one of the One Piece Mod's towns, now and then a small crew lands on the shore in a rowboat (or walks in from the edge) and marches on it, going after villagers, townsfolk, golems and you. A red boss bar counts the raiders left. Beat them all for Berries (and Hero of the Village in a village). Leave, or let six minutes pass, and they make off with their loot. About one raid per 25 minutes spent in a settlement, then a 20 minute break; while one is on, the main mod holds back its Marine hunts on you.
- Operators can call them with `/opbosses hunters [player]`, `/opbosses roam` and `/opbosses raid [player]`. Their kits are `wandering_hunter`, `veteran_hunter`, `rookie_raider` and `raider_captain` in `src/main/resources/opbosses/world_mobs.json`.

### Rival pirate crews

Six original crews roam the land behind their captain: the Red Tusk Pirates (brawlers and axes), Gilded Gull Pirates (gunners), Ashen Wake Pirates (bombers), Thornback Pirates (swordsmen), Coral Fang Pirates (Fish-Men) and Howling Gale Pirates (snipers).

- **On the move.** A captain leads two to five crewmates a short way at a time, waiting for stragglers. They attack you, Marines and bounty hunters on sight, and when one of them starts a fight the whole crew piles in. The captain shouts their crew's battle cry when they close in.
- **Crew against crew.** Rival crews hate each other more than they hate you. Two crews that meet fight it out, and sometimes you'll arrive in the middle of a brawl already going. Join in or let them wear each other down. They never turn on their own crew, and they leave the main mod's pirates alone.
- **Treasure camps.** Some crews have made camp: a campfire with log seats, a tent in the crew's colour, their flag on a pole, barrels of plunder and a weapon rack. Their treasure chest sits in the tent (Berries, gold, emeralds, sometimes a diamond, a golden apple or a Rumor Poster). A camped crew never leaves or despawns; get past them to get the chest.
- **Beating the captain** raises your bounty (8M for the Red Tusk captain up to 15M for the Coral Fang) and the toughest crewmate left takes over as Acting Captain.
- About one crew every 20 minutes out in the Overworld (never while another crew is close by): roughly a third are camps and a quarter are two crews clashing. Operators can call them with `/opbosses crew [crew]`, `/opbosses crew camp [crew]` and `/opbosses crew clash`. The crews are the `rivals` list in `src/main/resources/opbosses/crews.json` (captain kit, crew kits, size, colour, battle cry); the spawn rates are at the top of `crew/RivalCrews.java`.

## Marine Bounty Board

A notice board the Marines pin their hunts to. Craft one (three blue wool over paper, planks, paper, on two sticks) or take it from the creative tab, and right-click it.

- Every Minecraft day the board shows **five fresh hunts**, the same on every board in the world: two **Wanted rogues** (one always from tiers 1 to 3) and three jobs against roaming mobs, one of each: **bring down pirates** (any pirate NPC: sea pirates, rogue crews, raiders), **break up a rookie raiding crew** and **cull a creature** that's been attacking travellers (a bananawani, a manticore...).
- Click **[Take]** on one. One hunt at a time. Taking a rogue hunt hands you that rogue's Rumor Poster, so you can call them out (or find them in their lair). Everyone near a rogue when it falls gets the kill.
- When it's done, go back to any board and right-click it to get paid in **Berries** (45 to 230 for a rogue, 25 to 70 for the rest), plus a **rare drop** on some hunts: a tougher rogue's Rumor Poster, two diamonds, or a Lair Restoration Charter for a tier 4 or 5 rogue. The reward shows on the board before you take it.
- `/bountyboard` shows your hunt, `/bountyboard drop` gives it up. A hunt you took can't be taken again the same day.

## Building

The One Piece Mod isn't on a maven, so build against its jar, the same way as One Piece Arsenal:

1. In the One Piece Mod, run `./gradlew build`.
2. Copy `build/libs/onepiece-0.1.0.jar` into this repo's `libs/` folder (change `onepiece_jar` in `gradle.properties` if the name differs).
3. Run `./gradlew build` here. The jar lands in `build/libs/`; put it in your `mods/` folder next to the One Piece Mod.
4. To test, run `./gradlew runClientGameTest`. A game window opens, fights every boss in a test world and closes: Rumor Poster spawn, crew waves, phase lines, signature moves, trophy, bounty (including a long-range kill), the Hard rematch and Rumor Poster drops. It fails with a list of what went wrong, checks every boss's arena (lair music, boss bar, hazards, last wave), the bounty hunters, a pirate raid, the rival crews (a roaming crew, a clash and a treasure camp) and the Marine Bounty Board, and saves screenshots to `build/run/clientGameTest/screenshots`. The tests are in `src/gametest` and never go into the mod jar, and a normal `runClient` or modpack launch never runs them. To replay only what a change touches, add `-Dopbosses.tests=bosses`, `arenas`, `lairs`, `mobs`, `crews` or `bounty` (or `models` for model portraits) and `-Dopbosses.only=<boss ids or lair names>` (`hunters` or `raids` for the world mobs, `roam`, `clash` or `camp` for the crews).
